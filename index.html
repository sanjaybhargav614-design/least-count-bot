How to play

🎯 The goal

Finish each round with a lower hand total than the bot. Points are bad: you collect the opponent's total when you win a round, or a penalty when you get it wrong.

  • Each player gets 7 cards, and one card is turned face up to start the open pile.
  • The purple Joker card on the table sets the joker rank: every card of that rank is worth 0 this round.
  • The first player to reach points loses the game.

🃏 Card values

A♥1 point
2–9face value
10JQK10 points
🃏Jokers: 0 points
Joker rank0 points (changes each round)

Tip: your running total is shown under your hand, and it turns green when you are allowed to show.

🔁 Your turn

  1. Show (optional). If your total is or less, you may end the round right now. Otherwise continue.
  2. Drop. Select one or more cards of the same rank and drop them on the open pile.
  3. Pick. Take the card that was face up under your drop, or draw from the pile.

If the rank you drop matches the open card, you skip the pick. The bot sees which open cards you take, so use that to your advantage and watch what it takes too.
